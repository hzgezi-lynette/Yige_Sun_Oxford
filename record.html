Timeline

Dates and milestones. Awards are on the home page and funding is on the Projects page.

  • 2013M.Eng, Tsinghua University, China
  • 2013Research and development, Hehui Optoelectronics, Shanghai, China
  • 2014 to 2017PhD student (University of Tsukuba / NIMS, Japan) and Junior Researcher at NIMS
    Thesis: Fabrication and Structural Control of Graphene-based Electrode Materials in Lithium-ion Capacitors and Batteries. Investigated the structural and electrochemical differences between graphite oxide and graphene oxide, clarifying their distinct reduction behaviours as anode precursors; developed graphene-based electrodes with tailored interlayer spacing using carbon nanotubes and 2D MoS2, enabling dual-ion (Li/Na) storage. Four patents licensed to Murata Manufacturing; JST programme on graphene capacitor devices. NIMS Graduate Research Assistantship (GBP 80,000); Nanotechnology Students' Summer School in Australia, with the Best Presentation Award (2015) and a GBP 4,000 award (2016).
  • 2017 to 2018Postdoctoral researcher, NIMS, Japan
