# Dr Yige Sun: personal academic website

Website: https://hzgezi-lynette.github.io/Yige_Sun_Oxford/

Research Fellow, Linacre College, University of Oxford. Faraday Institution Research Fellow, Department of Materials, University of Oxford.

Research on measurable, automated and circular batteries: electrode manufacturing, chemistry-resolved measurement, robotics and automation, and circular design for batteries and metals on Earth and in space.

## Repository structure

- `*.html`: the pages above (static HTML, no build step)
- `assets/`: stylesheet, script and images
- `404.html`: fallback page
- `.nojekyll`: tells GitHub Pages to serve the files as they are
